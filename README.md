# AI Job Market Analysis: Automation vs. Traditional Roles

## Project Overview

This project explores the 2026 AI job market using Python and pandas to identify trends in AI-related and traditional employment opportunities.

The analysis examines job titles, salaries, locations, employment types, job categories, and AI-related classifications to better understand how artificial intelligence is influencing the job market.

## Project Objectives

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

- How do salary ranges compare across different positions?
- Which locations contain the most AI-related job opportunities?
- What types of jobs appear most frequently in the AI job market?
- What patterns can be identified within AI-related and traditional roles?

## Dataset

The project uses the **AI Job Market 2026: Automation vs. Traditional Role** dataset.

The dataset contains approximately 1,700 job postings and includes information about job titles, companies, locations, job categories, employment types, salary ranges, and AI-related classifications.
